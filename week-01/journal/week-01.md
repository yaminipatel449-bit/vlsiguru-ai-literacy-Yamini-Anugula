# Weekly Reflection — Week 01

## 1. Key Insights & Takeaways
- **AI Taxonomy:** AI is the overarching umbrella, ML learns patterns from data, DL handles complex unstructured data via neural networks, GenAI creates content, and AI Agents act autonomously using tools and planning loops.
- **Deterministic vs. Probabilistic:** Traditional software runs fixed human-coded rules (`if/else`), while AI relies on statistical probabilities trained on data.
- **LLM Mechanics:** Language models do not "think" or check facts—they perform next-token prediction based on statistical patterns learned during training.
- **The Need for Verification:** AI models sound confident regardless of accuracy. Technical outputs must always be cross-checked against authoritative primary sources.

## 2. Practical Application & Impact
- Developing a **Personal AI Verification Protocol** provides a structured way to catch subtle hallucinations before using AI outputs in engineering tasks.
- Understanding the boundary between basic LLM chat and Agentic workflows changes how I approach automating multi-step tasks.
